Density conversion
==================

Compare bilinear and Schneider HU-to-density conversions. Requires NumPy and
Matplotlib; no simulation runs.

Download the script: :download:`plot_06_schneider_density_conversion.py
</examples/getting_started/plot_06_schneider_density_conversion.py>`.

.. figure:: /_static/examples/density_conversion/density_conversion.png
   :alt: Bilinear and Schneider density curves and their differences
   :width: 100%

   Figure 1. Density curves and their differences over the full and clinical HU
   ranges.

The models differ most in lung, bone and metal. Their difference is not a
measurement of either model's accuracy.
